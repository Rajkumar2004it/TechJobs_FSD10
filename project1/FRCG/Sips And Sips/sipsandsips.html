Sips & Sips (Drinks)

Drinks, smoothies, shakes & coolers

Mango Lassi

    Ingredients :

  • 1 cup mango pulp
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup cold water or milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar (optional)
  • A pinch of cardamom powder (optional)

    Making Process :

  1. Blend mango pulp, yogurt, water/milk, sugar, and cardamom till smooth.
  2. Serve chilled.

Chaas (Buttermilk)

    Ingredients :

  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 1/2 cup cold water
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1-2 curry leaves (optional)
  • Fresh coriander, chopped (optional)
  • Salt to taste

    Making Process :

  1. Whisk yogurt with water and salt until frothy.
  2. Add cumin powder, curry leaves, and coriander. Mix well.
  3. Serve chilled.

Cold Coffee

    Ingredients :

  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 2 tsp instant coffee powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • 2 tbsp vanilla ice cream (optional)

    Making Process :

  1. Blend coffee powder, sugar, and a little milk to dissolve.
  2. add remaining milk and ice cubes, blend again.
  3. Pour in glass, top with ice cream if desired. Serve cold.

Masala Soda

    Ingredients :

  • 1 cup chilled soda water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/4 tsp black salt
  • A pinch of roasted cumin powder
  • Ice cubes
  • Mint leaves for garnish

    Making Process :

  1. Mix lemon juice, chaat masala, black salt, and cumin powder.
  2. Add soda water and ice cubes. Stir gently.
  3. Garnish with mint leaves and serve immediately.
