Hot and Sour Soup

    Ingredients:

    • 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
    • 1 cup mushrooms (sliced)
    • 1/2 cup shredded carrots
    • 1/4 cup bamboo shoots (optional)
    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 tbsp vinegar
    • 1 tsp chili sauce
    • 1/2 tsp black pepper
    • 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water (slurry)
    • 1 egg (lightly beaten)
    • Salt to taste
    • Chopped spring onions (for garnish)

Preparation Steps:

  1. Bring broth to a boil in a pot.
  2. Add mushrooms, carrots, bamboo shoots, and let it simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  3. Stir in soy sauce, vinegar, chili sauce, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add cornstarch slurry while stirring to thicken the soup.
  5. Slowly pour in the beaten egg while stirring gently to create egg ribbons.
  6. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve hot.

Dumpings

    Ingredients:

    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • ¾ cup warm water
    • 1 cup finely chopped cabbage
    • ½ cup finely chopped carrots
    • ½ cup finely chopped onions
    • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
    • 1 inch ginger (grated)
    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • Salt and pepper to taste
    • 1 tbsp oil (for cooking filling)

Preparation Steps:

  1. In a bowl, mix flour and warm water to make a smooth dough. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic, ginger, onions, carrots, and cabbage.
  3. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Cook the mixture for 5–7 minutes. Let it cool.
  4. Divide dough into small balls, roll them into thin circles.
  5. Place a spoon of filling in the center, fold, and seal the edges to form dumplings.
  6. Steam the dumplings for 10–12 minutes or until translucent.
  7. Serve hot with dipping sauce.

Szechwan Chilli Chicken

Ingredients:

  • 500g boneless chicken (cut into small pieces)
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp black pepper powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for deep frying
  • 1 medium onion (sliced)
  • 1 bell pepper (sliced)
  • 2 green chilies (slit)
  • 1 tbsp garlic (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp ginger (chopped)
  • 2–3 tbsp Schezwan sauce
  • 1 tbsp red chili sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Spring onions for garnish

Preparation Steps:

  1. In a bowl, mix chicken pieces with cornflour, maida, egg, soy sauce, pepper, and salt. Marinate for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil and deep fry the chicken until golden and crisp. Set aside.
  3. In another pan, heat 2 tbsp oil. Sauté garlic, ginger, green chilies, onion, and bell pepper for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add Schezwan sauce, chili sauce, tomato ketchup, vinegar, and sugar. Mix well and cook for a minute.
  5. Add the fried chicken to the sauce and toss everything together to coat well.
  6. Cook for 2 more minutes. Garnish with chopped spring onions.
  7. Serve hot with fried rice or noodles.

Veg Hakka Noodles

    Ingredients:

    • 200g Hakka noodles
    • 1 cup shredded cabbage
    • 1/2 cup julienned carrots
    • 1/2 cup sliced capsicum
    • 1/2 cup chopped spring onions
    • 2 tbsp oil
    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 tbsp chili sauce
    • 1/2 tbsp vinegar
    • 1 tsp black pepper
    • Salt to taste
    • 1 tsp chopped garlic

Preparation Steps:

    1. Boil noodles as per instructions. Rinse with cold water and toss with 1 tsp oil. Set aside.
    2. Heat oil in a wok, add garlic and sauté for 30 seconds.
    3. Add spring onions, cabbage, carrots, and capsicum. Stir-fry on high heat for 2–3 minutes.
    4. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
    5. Add the cooked noodles and toss everything together on high flame for 2 minutes.
    6. Garnish with spring onion greens and serve hot.
